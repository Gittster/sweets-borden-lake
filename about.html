Our Mission

The Sweet's Borden Lake Association exists to protect and preserve the natural beauty of Borden Lake, promote responsible stewardship of our shared shoreline, and foster a strong sense of community among all who live on and visit our lake.

We hold regular meetings open to all lakeside property owners and welcome active participation from every household on the lake.
